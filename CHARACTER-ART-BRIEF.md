# Character Portrait Brief — *The Reverse of the Cloth* (Signal-Weave)

A hand-off for **Claude Design**. Pair this with visual inspiration references and it should
have everything needed to produce the cast portraits. Everything here is drawn from the
actual game data (cast, families, roles, sigils) and the game's existing look.

---

## 1. The look (art direction)

- **Setting:** a decadent, ancient **star-empire** in decline. Its dynasty and law are not
  written but **woven as light** — "the Weave." Every person is recorded as a **holographic
  bust** floating in an ornate frame on a retro-futuristic terminal.
- **Style:** retro-futuristic / **holographic / CRT**. Think scanlines, soft bloom/glow, a
  faint chromatic shimmer, light drawn as fine woven threads. Ornate but a little decayed —
  a grand old empire gone to seed.
- **Format:** square portrait (1:1), head-and-shoulders bust, transparent or dark-violet
  background (`#0a0712`) so it sits in the game's holographic node frame. Leave a little
  headroom for the frame.
- **The frame = a sigil ("nael").** Each character is bound to a short **knot-pattern**
  (their nael — see each entry). Render it as a tidy row of vertical knot-marks worked into
  the **base or border of the frame** — a unique signature per portrait, and a nod to the
  cipher mechanic. (Naels are given where the game fixes them; invent simple 3-knot patterns
  for the rest, all visually distinct.)
- **CRUCIAL — grayscale first, colour on reveal.** In game, every portrait starts **black &
  white** and snaps to **full family colour the moment the character is identified**. So:
  - Design each portrait to read **in B&W** — strong silhouette, clear value contrast,
    legible props and expression with no colour at all.
  - The **family colour is the payoff** of the reveal — it should feel like the character
    "lights up." One dominant family hue per portrait (a holographic tint), with the game's
    accent colours used sparingly.
- **Consistency:** shared frame language, lighting direction, and bust framing across the
  whole cast. Vary by **family colour + role iconography + personal features/age**.

### Two conventions to keep families readable

- **Married-in (a spouse who joined the house):** take the **spouse's family colour**, but add
  a small "joined" marker — e.g. a single off-colour band in the frame, or a half-frame —
  so they read as *attached to*, not *born of*, the line.
- **Outsiders / `shen` (foreigners who married or loved into the house):** family colour at
  the core, but edged in the **outsider orange** (`#ff9a3c`) and given a **broken / dashed
  frame** — they belong, but the empire never fully accepted them.

## 2. The colour key, by family

> "Family" = a bloodline branch, each its own colour. Court, Servants, and Foreign Powers are
> the three non-family groups (their own colours too). Collapse or rename any of these as you
> like — the groupings below are the natural ones in the story.

1. **The Founders** — gold `#ffd56b` (Vethra, Orel, + the lost infant Eddan)
2. **The Seated Line / Marenn's branch** — purple `#b06cff` (Marenn, Hadon, Edra, Pol, Solenne, Renor, Wenla, Avesa)
3. **The Rightful Line / Suvi's branch** — green `#54ffb0` (Suvi, Talis*, Nemora, Dris*)  — *outsiders, orange-edged

5. **The Servants** — pewter/bronze `#9a8c7a` (Dav, Tomas, Yola)

## 3. The cast

Each entry: **Name "title"** · family/colour · role · **Portrait** notes. Where a character has
a public *front* and a hidden *truth*, both are noted — see the **spoiler note** in §4 about how
visible to make the "tells."

### The Founders — gold `#ffd56b`

- **Vethra "the First Warp"** · founder, the first **Warden of the Loom** (the ruler).
  **Portrait:** the eldest matriarch; severe, magnificent, founding-mother gravitas. Crowned
  with a **warp-diadem** — a spindle/loom-crown of taut light-threads. Fine threads radiate
  outward from her like a loom strung from her shoulders (she is the root of the whole Weave).
  Tall collar, vertical silhouette, unbroken gaze. The most "regal" of all.
- **Orel "the Loomfoot"** · *married-in* consort. "Stood at the loom but did not hold it."
  **Portrait:** dignified but deliberately secondary — gaze a touch lowered/aside, hands folded
  at rest. A **footed-loom** motif at the base of the frame. Quieter regalia than Vethra; the
  married-in band. Kindly, content to support.
- **Eddan "the Short Strand"** · Vethra's third child, a son, **died in infancy** (the "lost
  thread"). **Portrait:** a small child / near-infant, rendered **faded and unfinished** — the
  hologram thins to nothing at the edges. A single **short, cut thread** closed with a
  **dark-knot** (the mark of the gone). Tender, ghostly, barely there.

### The Seated Line (Marenn's branch) — imperial purple `#b06cff`

- **Marenn "the Standing Warp"** · *Front:* Vethra's eldest, the 2nd Warden. *Truth:* truly the
  **second**-born — her line holds the throne by pushing the true elder (Suvi) aside.
  **Portrait:** imposing, upright, chin high; a diadem like Vethra's but worn slightly **askew**,
  or subtly a **copy** of it. Optional tell: a faint **second silhouette** echoing behind her —
  the primacy she took. Proud, certain.
- **Hadon "the Unmarked"** · *married-in*. Plain, unremarkable. **Portrait:** the most
  understated face in the cast — where others carry ornament, his sigil-panel is **blank**.
  Plain collar, even features, married-in band. Pleasant but forgettable (the joke is in his
  title).
- **Edra "the Loomwright"** · Marenn's eldest; the 3rd Warden; **childless**. **Portrait:** a
  maker/engineer of the Weave — a wright's harness or apron of light, fine tools at hand,
  ruler's diadem. The thread that should descend from her simply **ends** (no heir). Capable,
  craftsman's focus.
- **Pol "the Quiet Hand"** · *married-in* to Edra. **Portrait:** reserved; **hands prominent and
  perfectly still** ("the quiet hand"). Soft-spoken posture, married-in band.
- **Solenne "the Even Hand"** · Nemora's godmother. *Front:* the wise, even-handed 4th Warden
  who "steadied the house." *Truth:* the **usurper** — but a **puppet**, installed and directed
  from outside; she ordered the heir's removal. **Portrait:** the most double-faced. Serene,
  balanced, maternal poise; a **scales/balance** motif; ruler's diadem. Optional tell: a few
  near-invisible **strings/threads rising off the top of the frame** (she is worked from above),
  and one cold **ember-crimson thread** (the Factor's colour) tangled into her purple — the only
  warm-cold discord in her otherwise calm image. Dangerous-under-calm.
- **Renor "the Brackish"** · Marenn's son, **passed over** for the throne; bitter; Avesa's
  father. **Portrait:** soured, "brackish" — a downturned, resentful set to the mouth; an empty
  **diadem-shadow** hovering just out of his reach (the crown he never got). Glowers.
- **Wenla "the Farwife"** · *married-in* to Renor; "came from afar." **Portrait:** a slightly
  foreign-but-within-empire air; travelled, watchful; married-in band. Supporting presence.
- **Avesa "the Loud Loom"** · Renor's daughter. *Front/Reverse:* the **loud rival** who openly,
  brashly claims the throne — looks guiltiest of anyone. *Truth:* the **patsy**, framed and
  later cleared. **Portrait:** brassy, mid-shout — **mouth open in a claim, both hands thrust
  out** grasping for a crown; ostentatious, attention-grabbing regalia ("the loud loom").
  Optional tell: a **forged sigil** pinned to her that is *almost but not quite* Solenne's nael
  — the frame-up. Young, ambitious, impossible to ignore. (The red herring — she should *read*
  guilty.)

### The Rightful Line (Suvi's branch) — luminous green `#54ffb0`

- **Suvi "the Silent Relay"** · *Front:* Vethra's second daughter who "wove but did not rule."
  *Truth:* the **true firstborn**, set aside for loving an outsider; the **silenced weaver** who
  authored the whole reverse; **Nemora's mother**; imprisoned, gone dark. **Portrait:** the
  emotional heart. A weaver at her work — a **relay/shuttle of light** in hand, fine threads
  trailing from her fingers (she is literally weaving the hidden truth). A **silenced** motif:
  a finger to the lips, or a thread drawn across a closed mouth. Sorrowful, dignified, eyes that
  seem to look *through* you. Optional: a faint **mirrored double** behind her (she speaks from
  the reverse / the underside), and a dimming at the edges (gone dark / imprisoned). The most
  poignant face in the game.
- **Talis "the Stranger"** · *outsider (`shen`)* — Suvi's secret husband, Nemora's father, gone
  dark before her; appears only on the reverse. **Portrait:** clearly **off-world / foreign** in
  feature and dress — a traveller's mantle, ornament from somewhere else entirely. Green core,
  **outsider orange edge, broken/dashed frame**. Warm, gentle, a little weary from the road.
  Subtle: a **twined double-thread / key** motif (his name is half of the deepest cipher key).
  Dimmed (gone dark).
- **Nemora "the Unwoven"** · Suvi + Talis's daughter — **the rightful heir**. *Front:* a nameless
  ward, "dead young of fever." *Truth:* trueborn, acknowledged then **erased**; **murdered**
  (cut out of turn). **Portrait:** the central tragedy. A luminous young woman, quietly bright,
  quick-eyed. Her hologram is visibly **coming unwoven** — threads loosening and trailing off
  at one edge, part of her image **erased/incomplete** (she was cut from the record). A
  half-erased **first-knot** at her collar (the knot her mother tied to name her), and a clean
  **severed thread** at the throat or heart (she was mis-shorn). She should look the most
  *interrupted* of anyone — beautiful and unfinished. On reveal, her restored name could glow.
- **Dris "the Farcomer"** · *outsider (`shen`)* — Nemora's secret lover; the **witness** who
  saved the cords and fled, which is the only reason the truth survives. **Portrait:** a young
  outsider/courier, road-worn and earnest; a **runner's** energy (he fled the plateau). A
  **bundle of cords/data wound around one arm** — the evidence he carried out. Green core,
  outsider orange edge, dashed frame. Loyal, brave, a bit wild. The keeper of the truth.

### The Court (household officials, not blood) — cool blue `#4fd6ff`

- **Caleth "the Patient" · nael `[1,5,2]`** · Nemora's tutor (pattern-master). *Truth:* the
  **assassin** ("the shears") who did the killing. **Portrait:** outwardly the gentle, patient
  teacher — books/pattern-master tools, a calm and kindly face. The tell (keep it *subtle* and
  deniable): a pair of **shear-blades** half-hidden in the silhouette — folded into a high
  collar, or glinting at the base of the frame. The patience of a predator.
- **Tovesh "the Counter" · nael `[3,1,4]`** · the **Karna** (treasurer/keeper of the cords).
  *Truth:* the **paymaster** — but a conduit, and so compulsively honest in his books that his
  records damn everyone, himself included. **Portrait:** a fastidious accountant draped in
  **knotted cords (karn)** — ledgers as physical cords looped over the shoulders, number-marks
  and a counting-cord/abacus of light. Precise, anxious-honest expression. The most
  "cord-covered" figure (he *is* the evidence).
- **Ilse "the Open Face" · nael `[4,1,3]`** · Nemora's sworn friend and companion. *Truth:* the
  **spy** — but **coerced**, her own infant held hostage over her. **Portrait:** a warm, frank,
  **open face** you instinctively trust — and behind it, hidden anguish. The tell: a small
  **held bundle / a tiny cradle-thread** she's forced to clutch (the child used against her),
  and a strained or tear-bright eye that doesn't match the open smile. Tragic and loving — the
  saddest of the court.

### The Servants (commoners) — warm pewter/bronze `#9a8c7a`

> Search-noise in the puzzle, but give them charm — each one a little trade vignette. Low
> saturation, humble garb, honest weathered faces; the bronze "below-stairs" tint unites them.

- **Dav "the Sifter"** · sifts the house's stores. **Portrait:** a **sieve** of light, motes of
  grain/data drifting through; rolled sleeves, patient squint.
- **Tomas "the Scourer"** · scours and cleans. **Portrait:** mid-scrub, a **scouring cloth/brush**
  of light, sleeves up, ruddy and brisk.
- **Yola "the Tinter"** · gives the pale strands their colour (a dyer). **Portrait:** **colour-
  stained hands**, a little palette of light-tints at her side — the one servant literally
  associated with colour (a nice irony for the B&W→colour reveal: let *her* be the most
  colourful when she lights up).

### The Foreign Powers (the Outside) — cold ember-crimson `#ff5d7a`

> Cold, off-world, threatening — the only crimson in the cast, so they read as *outside* the
> warm dynastic palette. Apex antagonists; reveal late.

- **the Factor "the Outside Counter" · nael `[7,3,7]`** · a foreign broker who **funded** the
  killing and **directed** Solenne from the shadows. **Portrait:** an alien financier — off-world
  attire, impersonal and cold; **foreign cords** in a different knot-style than the house's; a
  face **half-masked or shadowed** (he works through others, unknown until uncovered). Crimson.
  His portrait could begin the most *obscured* and resolve as he's named.
- **the Outloom "the Loom Beyond" · nael `[5,2,5]`** · the foreign power that **engineered the
  empire's fall to absorb it** — the prime mover; and (the final twist) the order that **hired
  the player.** **Portrait:** less a person than a **power**. Suggest a vast, cold **rival
  loom-engine** / a faceless crowned sovereign made of distant light / or a monumental **sigil**
  (its heir-nael `[5,2,5]` — the very seal stamped on the first document the player ever reads).
  The biggest, coldest, most abstract image in the set. Crimson, monumental, distant.

## 4. Notes for the designer

- **Spoiler-sensitivity of the "tells."** Portraits are visible from early on, and the game is a
  deduction puzzle. The optional villain/victim "tells" (Solenne's strings, Caleth's shears,
  Avesa's forged sigil, Marenn's doubled silhouette, Ilse's held child) should be **subtle and
  deniable** — the kind of thing you only notice *after* you know, not a label. If in doubt,
  make them ambiguous. (If you'd rather keep portraits totally "innocent" and add the tells only
  to a second, revealed state, that works too — say the word.)
- **One image or two?** The game currently shows **one** portrait per character (greyscale →
  colour). If you ever want a true front/reverse split (a public face vs a truer one) for the
  big three — Suvi, Nemora, Solenne — that's a lovely option, but not required.
- **What to send back:** square PNGs (transparent or `#0a0712` bg), designed to read in
  greyscale, with the family hue as the colour layer. Keeping the **nael knot-pattern** in each
  frame ties the art to the puzzle.
- **Cast count:** 20 named portraits (3 Founders, 8 Seated Line, 4 Rightful Line, 3 Court, 2
  Foreign Powers) + 3 Servants = **23 total**.
