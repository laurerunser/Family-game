About this Archive

THE REVERSE OF THE CLOTH · Signal-Weave

This is a small, patient game about reading a fallen house's records and giving its dead back their names. Laure Runser did the coding and the art — every portrait, sigil, and holographic frame painfully traced in SVG from various photos and collages. Rose Bertrand did the story: she wrote all the documents and their logic — the sealed Old Tongue, the hidden reverses and their steganography, the knotted cords and their ciphers, and the single solution they all resolve to. No AI use anywhere — not in the code, not in the words, not in the art.

With thanks

Great thanks to the little sisters who tested this to make sure it was decently difficult without being infuriating (there's nothing we love more than furious little sisters).

Great thanks, too, to the people who made the great game The Archives of Tresova — we shamelessly ripped off the game mechanics, but we added our own story, clues, and style to it. Hopefully it's a bit harder than the original (which was already decently complicated, taking about two hours to finish!). Here is also a blog post they wrote which lists their influences (we still need to go play all the games they listed as inspiration!): Updates, Inspirations, and what's next for The Archives of Trevosa.

— Laure Runser & Rose Bertrand
